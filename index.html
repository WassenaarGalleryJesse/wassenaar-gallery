      Buying

      Every piece is sold through 1stDibs

      This site is where you look around. When you've found something, the button on each piece takes you to its page on 1stDibs. There you can buy it, make an offer or send us a question.

      1. Payment & protection

        Checkout and payment happen securely on 1stDibs, and every purchase is covered by their buyer protection.

      2. Delivery included

        Every price includes delivery. We pack each piece ourselves in Lisse and send it to your door, anywhere in the world, at no extra cost.

      3. Questions & offers

        Use “Message seller” or “Make offer” on the 1stDibs page. Your message comes straight to us.

      4. Condition

        Everything here is old and has been used. Each listing describes its condition. If you want more photos or measurements, just ask.

      About

      Wassenaar Gallery

      We have been buying and selling antiques for over twenty years, from Lisse, in the bulb country between Haarlem and Leiden. Since 2016 we have sold through 1stDibs to buyers all over the world.

      What we look for is simple to say and hard to find: pieces made by good craftsmen of their time, in good condition and with lots of character left in them.
